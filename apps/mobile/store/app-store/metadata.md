## Description (4000)

Healuxa is a premium healthcare concierge for the UAE.

• Verified practitioners only — every doctor listed holds a current licence checked by our team
  (licensing authority and expiry shown on each profile).
• Transparent pricing — each service shows the total and its breakdown into the professional fee
  and the facility fee. No hidden referral commissions.
• Clinics and medical centres — see locations, opening hours and the services offered at each
  facility.
• Your language — English, Arabic and Persian, with full right-to-left layout.
• Private by design — your session is stored in the device Keychain, optionally protected with
  Face ID, and you can see and sign out every device signed in to your account.

Online booking is coming soon.

Healuxa does not provide medical advice or emergency services. In an emergency call 998 (UAE).
